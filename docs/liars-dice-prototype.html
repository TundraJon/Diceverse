Rules of Liar's Dice

Goal: Be the last player remaining with dice!

1. Rolling: Every player secretly rolls 5 dice under their cup.

2. Bidding: Players take turns making higher bids on how many dice of a face value exist across ALL hidden cups combined.

3. Wild 1s (Aces): 1s count as wild cards for any die face bid, unless 1s themselves are bid.

4. Escalating Bids: You must raise either the quantity (e.g. four 3s → five 3s) or the 2 face value (e.g. four 3s → four 4s).

5. Calling "Liar": If you believe the previous player's bid is higher than the true count on the table, call LIAR! All cups reveal.

If the bidder was wrong, they lose 1 die. If they were right, the caller loses 1 die!

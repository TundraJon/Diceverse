# High Roller: rules sheet

(In our own words. The game is a casino dice game of majorities; the names and art are ours.)

**Setup.** Six casinos numbered 1 to 6 (in the app: Lucky Seven, Golden Dragon, Silver Spur, Neon Palms, Crimson Ace, Blue Lagoon). 54 banknotes: 5 each of $60,000, $70,000, $80,000, $90,000; 6 each of $10,000, $40,000, $50,000; 8 each of $20,000 and $30,000, shuffled into a pile. Before each round every casino gets notes from the top of the pile until it holds at least $50,000. Everybody has 8 dice in their colour. The start player is chosen by the seed; the start card moves one seat on every round.

**A turn.** Roll ALL the dice you have left. Choose exactly ONE number among the dice rolled and put ALL the dice showing it on that casino (never only some of them). Then it is the next player's turn. A player with no dice left is skipped, so the last player with dice can take several turns in a row.

**Payout** (when every die has been placed), casino 1 to 6:
1. Players with the same number of dice at a casino cancel each other out: they get nothing from it.
2. Of the others, the most dice takes the highest note, the next most the next highest, and so on. Players without a note get nothing.
3. Notes nobody wins go under the pile. Won notes are kept (the money).

**End.** After the last round (4 by default; 3 or 5 as a house rule) the most money wins. A tie goes to the most banknotes; still tied means a shared win.

**Hidden money.** Notes being won are seen by everybody, but nobody shows a total until the end. In the app your own money is shown against bots; in Pass & Play nobody's is shown and the player in turn may hold a button to peek at theirs; everything is revealed at the end.

**Neutral dice** (house rule, Auto/Off/On; Auto = on with 2 players). White dice count as one extra imaginary player. With 2 players each player also gets 4 white dice, with 3 or 4 players 2 each (with 3 players the 2 spare white dice are rolled by themselves at the start of the round and placed on their numbers). White dice are rolled with your own on your turn, and when you choose a number the white dice showing it go with yours. At the payout the white player is scored like anybody else, and the notes it wins go under the pile. In Solo you play against 8 white dice and your score is your money.

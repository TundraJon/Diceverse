# Bot lines: what fires when

This is every spoken line, by bot. **Your decisions are in the last column (DEFAULT lines) or the Notes column.** Nothing here has been changed.

## When each group fires (today)

| Group | Fires when | Said how |
|---|---|---|
| THINKING | the whole turn of a bot | status text under the table ("Nat is reading the probability charts..."), not a speech bubble |
| DEFAULT | a bot's "talk turn" (every 2nd or 3rd turn of its own), now at the START of its turn | speech bubble, 3.5 s |
| NERVOUS | the first roll with a lot at stake (Pig 20+ turn points, Farkle 1000+, Cliffhanger all 3 markers, Space Salvage one hit from ending, Shut the Box 6 or less open) | speech bubble |
| SHOCKED | a bust, farkle, blown turn, no move, lost dice or penalty | speech bubble |
| WINNING | a big bank (Pig 20+, Farkle 1000+, ...), a column won, box shut, challenge won, row locked, round won, a big Yacht score, and game win | speech bubble |
| RELIEVED | a bank after being nervous | speech bubble |
| Game over | the winner says WINNING, or a bot says SHOCKED if a human wins | always said |

Only one bubble at a time; a bot says one line from its list at random without repeating until all are used.

## Summary of the problems I found

1. DEFAULT mixes two kinds of lines: before-I-roll lines (most of them) and hand-off lines to the next player (Pip "Your turn!", Snake "Your move.", Deuce "Take your turn, earthling.").
2. Pip, Snake and Deuce have only 2 DEFAULT lines each; splitting them leaves 1 opener and 1 hand-off, which would repeat every time. I would write 1 or 2 more of each (my addition, in their voice).
3. Deuce "Greetings, human." reads as a game-start greeting.
4. Nat uses the same line in WINNING and RELIEVED ("Ho! Ha ha! Guard! Turn! Parry! Dodge! Spin! Ha! Thrust!").
5. Dottie "Thermocouple engaged." (RELIEVED) is odd: I could not tell what it refers to.
6. Snake RELIEVED has only 1 line, so it repeats whenever he is relieved.
